# GameBox quick gameplay guide

## Where the games run

Original mini-games now open in the same arcade player as the other GameBox games. The play area stays centered, with recommended games alongside it on desktop and beneath it on narrow screens. The mini-game's title and controls stay with the board; its catalog and ad placeholders are hidden inside the embedded game so they cannot squeeze or scroll the play area.

## Controls and goals

All 42 mini-games show their own objective and controls above the board. They use these game rules:

| Game style | Goal | Controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tap and pop | Hit glowing targets; avoid decoys and beat the 30-second clock. | Mouse or touch |
| Catch | Catch falling good items and avoid decoys. | Move with mouse, touch, arrow keys, or A/D |
| Dodge | Collect falling items, avoid hazards, and survive the round. Three hits end the run. | Mouse, touch, arrow keys, or A/D |
| Memory | Repeat the growing sequence to clear the rounds. | Click or tap the highlighted tiles |
| Match | Find six pairs by turning over two cards at a time. | Click or tap |
| Timing | Score ten taps while the marker crosses the green target. | Space, Enter, mouse, or touch |
| Reaction | Wait for the green signal and react eight times; an early press ends the run. | Space, Enter, mouse, or touch |
| Arrow Escape Puzzle | Rotate arrows clockwise to guide the beam from START to EXIT. | Click or tap an arrow tile |

Best scores are stored in the player's browser on that device. They are not shared between players or devices.

## Partner titles

Arrow Exit Puzzle and Arrows: Escape - Tap Puzzle already appear in the selected Playgama catalog when that catalog loads. They are partner games, not GameBox originals. Other named games need to be present in the partner's authorized export or separately licensed before they can be listed; this site does not scrape or copy their game files.

## Ads and player flow

The ad spaces remain placeholders until valid ad units are configured. They sit outside the active game board. The site does not force a five-to-ten-minute ad before play. Google H5 Games Ads provides full-screen ads at natural breaks, such as between levels, through its Ad Placement API; participation and integration are required. See [Google's H5 Games Ads guidance](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9959170?hl=en) and [AdSense guidance for game pages](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/2768340?hl=en).
