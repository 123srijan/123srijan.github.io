Turbo Drift

A neon arcade race. Hold FORWARD to drive, BACKWARD to reverse, and LEFT / RIGHT to steer.

How to Play

Move: Hold FORWARD or W / Up. Hold BACKWARD or S / Down to slow, then reverse. Hold LEFT / RIGHT or A / D to steer.

Boost: Hold BOOST, Space, or Shift for a burst of speed. The blue meter refills while you drive without boost.

Reverse: Hold BACKWARD; it brakes first, then reverses automatically. Or tap R near a stop to select Reverse. AUTO shifts for you; MANUAL lets you use gears 1–5.

Cross the checkered line to complete a lap. Grass slows you down, and collisions cost speed. Escape or PAUSE stops the race.
