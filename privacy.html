Game progress

Star Patrol, Prism Path, Snake Run, Block Workshop, Tower Builder, Cricket Cup, and Firefly Frenzy save high scores or level progress in your browser’s local storage so your progress stays on your device. This site does not send that game progress to its owner.

This site uses Google Analytics to measure visits and understand how people use the site. Google Analytics may collect information such as pages viewed, browser and device details, approximate location, and interactions, and may use cookies or similar technologies. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services and read the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising and Google services

This site includes Google AdSense code and may display Google ads. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, local storage, IP addresses, or similar identifiers to deliver, personalize, limit, and measure ads. Personalized ads may be based on activity on this and other sites. You can manage Google's ad personalization in Google Ads Settings and review additional opt-out choices at aboutads.info. Google explains how it uses information from partner sites.

Where required, visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland must be shown a compliant consent choice before personal data or device storage is used for personalized advertising. Google's certified consent message can be configured in the AdSense account under Privacy & messaging. The site's owner is responsible for enabling the appropriate message and consent settings before serving ads in those regions.

Games

GameBox Arcade games are hosted on this website. The site stores some game progress and preferences in your browser's local storage; this information stays on your device unless a game or browser feature separately transmits it. Partner games are supplied by Playgama and its game partners and may load in an embedded frame. When you open a partner game, your browser connects to those providers, who may receive your IP address and browser/device information and may use their own cookies or similar technologies under their own privacy policies. Partner game content is provided by Playgama and its game partners.

Your choices and questions

You can manage Google's ad personalization using the settings linked above and control or delete cookies and local storage through your browser. Blocking storage may affect saved game progress. For questions, access, correction, or deletion requests about information handled by this site, contact the owner through the project repository. Requests about Google or partner-provider data should be directed to those providers using their privacy pages.
