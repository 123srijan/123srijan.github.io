Game progress

Star Patrol, Prism Path, Snake Run, Block Workshop, Tower Builder, and Cricket Cup save high scores or level progress in your browser’s local storage so your progress stays on your device. This site does not send that game progress to its owner.

Advertising and Google services

This site uses Google AdSense code and may show Google ads. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar identifiers to serve and measure ads, including ads based on visits to this or other sites. You can review how Google uses information from partner sites and manage personalization in Google Ads Settings. You can also visit aboutads.info choices.

Games

Original GameBox Arcade games are hosted on this website. Playgama partner games are supplied by Playgama and game partners and run in an embedded frame on a page on this website. Your browser connects to Playgama and the game provider to load those games; they may receive standard connection information such as your IP address and browser/device details, and may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. The partner-game player page does not load Google AdSense ads. Partner game content is provided by Playgama and its game partners.
